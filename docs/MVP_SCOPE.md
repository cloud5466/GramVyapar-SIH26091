# MVP scope

## Inputs

- Location
- Business
- Available Capital

## Outputs

- Local Market Indicators
- Business Potential
- Simple Financial Structure
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Next Steps

## Initial business categories

- Dairy
- Tailoring
- Kirana

## Initial locations

**TBD — exactly two demo locations.**

A location is not approved until the data lead documents available population,
mapped-business and local-context evidence and the technical lead accepts the
coverage limitations.

## Out of scope for the first MVP

- Authentication
- Loan approval
- Aadhaar integration
- Bank integration
- Custom ML training
- Nationwide live business coverage
- Blockchain
- Complex forecasting
- Full mobile app

## Scope guardrails

- The product provides indicative decision support, not a sanction decision.
- The MVP may demonstrate only the three approved business categories.
- Unknown locations and unsupported categories must be handled explicitly.
- Illustrative values may be used for UI demonstration only when clearly
  labelled.
- Regional-language and voice support may be described as future work but are
  not assumed to be implemented.

## MVP completion criteria

- Two demo locations approved with source records.
- Three business profiles reviewed.
- At least one official financial rule implemented and independently checked.
- Viability formulas, ranges and rating thresholds documented and tested.
- One golden demo case traceable end to end.
- Confidence/source detail visible in the result.
- Failure cases and mobile flow tested.
- Every presentation claim recorded in the claim audit.
