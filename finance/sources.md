## Verification checklist

- [ ] Publisher identity confirmed
- [ ] Current version or effective date confirmed
- [ ] Relevant clause/page recorded
- [ ] Values transcribed without changing units
- [ ] Boundary conditions captured
- [ ] Superseded guidance identified
- [ ] Independent review completed
