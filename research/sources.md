## Source review

- Prefer the official SIH statement for problem requirements.
- Prefer original research papers over summaries.
- Record study limitations and geographic context.
- Mark unverified claims as open assumptions, not facts.
- Cross-reference slide claims in `testing/claim_audit.md`.
