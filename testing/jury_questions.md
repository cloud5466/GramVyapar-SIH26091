# Jury questions

Prepare concise answers supported by repository files. Record open questions
instead of inventing an answer.

## Problem Understanding

- Which entrepreneur decision does GramVyapar improve first?
- Why is hyper-local evidence necessary for this problem?
- What is explicitly outside the MVP?

## Data

- Which sources are official, open, mapped, user-provided or illustrative?
- How are competitor counts and customer-reach proxies produced?
- What happens when local data is incomplete or stale?

## Finance

- Which values are calculated programmatically?
- How are official rules verified and versioned?
- How do you distinguish indicative financing from loan eligibility or sanction?

## AI

- What does the AI layer do, and what is it prohibited from calculating?
- How is the explanation constrained by sourced evidence?
- How will hallucinations or unsupported claims be detected?

## Technical Architecture

- What are the inputs and outputs of each engine?
- How are data contracts enforced?
- How can a result be reproduced or audited?

## Scalability

- How do new locations, businesses and schemes enter the system?
- Which parts scale horizontally and which require expert review?
- How does the two-location MVP demonstrate the larger architecture honestly?

## User Experience

- Why is the public UI simpler than the underlying architecture?
- How will low digital and financial literacy be supported?
- What happens for an unknown location or unsupported business?

## Ethics / Trust

- How are evidence type and confidence communicated?
- How does the product avoid overstating financial eligibility?
- What user-provided data is necessary, optional or sensitive?

## Validation

- What has been tested with real users or domain experts?
- Which claims are validated today and which remain hypotheses?
- What is the backup plan if data or AI services fail during the demo?
