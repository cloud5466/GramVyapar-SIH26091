# Slide 2 — Problem + GramVyapar Solution

## PURPOSE

Connect the entrepreneur's decision problem to the product's simple input and
output flow.

## MAIN MESSAGE

Three understandable inputs become an evidence-backed local business view,
financial structure and next-step plan.

## VISUAL

Location + Business Idea + Available Capital → GramVyapar → Business
Potential + Financial Structure + Next Steps.

## CONTENT

- Problem: local demand, competition and finance guidance are fragmented.
- Inputs: location, proposed business and available capital.
- Outputs: local market indicators, business potential, risks, opportunities,
  simple financial structure and recommended actions.
- Public experience remains simple; technical detail is progressively disclosed.

## DATA REQUIRED

Final two MVP demo locations and approved golden-demo business scenario.

## SOURCE REQUIRED

SIH requirement mapping, user-persona evidence and approved product scope.

## CURRENT STATUS

Core flow ready; research-backed problem framing remains to be completed.
