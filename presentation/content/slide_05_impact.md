# Slide 5 — Impact + Example User Journey

## PURPOSE

Demonstrate the practical decision support an entrepreneur receives without
claiming unmeasured outcomes.

## MAIN MESSAGE

The product helps an entrepreneur understand the local opportunity, money
picture, risks and first actions before committing capital.

## VISUAL

One approved golden-demo journey from three inputs to a simple result and three
recommended next steps.

## CONTENT

- Conceptual outcomes: informed entrepreneurship, reduced decision risk,
  accessible financial understanding and scalable rural advisory.
- Example result must be labelled illustrative until supported by real evidence.
- Avoid fabricated impact percentages or loan-approval claims.

## DATA REQUIRED

Approved golden-demo inputs, sourced local evidence, deterministic outputs and
usability observations.

## SOURCE REQUIRED

Golden-demo handoff, engine output, user validation notes and claim audit.

## CURRENT STATUS

Conceptual impact framing ready; evidence-backed example is pending.
